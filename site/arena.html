When the shape is still open

Use Arena for a homepage direction, an architecture, or another substantial artifact with several plausible approaches. A routine change rarely needs competing candidates.

Choose, then synthesize

Candidate workers explore in parallel, each in its own scratch folder. After they finish, a blind judge sees only the criteria and the anonymized work, then checks each candidate against them. The manager reads every candidate, chooses a base, and adapts one or two ideas from each of the others.

What you keep

One synthesized artifact and a note listing each worker, the judge’s verdict, the base and why, every idea taken or left, and the check results. Candidates stay in .tmp/arena/.

What it needs

Fresh-context candidate workers, isolated output paths, and a fresh judge. With a ChatGPT-subscription Codex login, one candidate and the judge run on Codex by default. Review starts after candidate writing stops, and the whole run gets one rerun. The combined result still needs verification; agreement is not a pass.

The example, without playback

Three candidates answer the same homepage brief. B becomes the base for its hierarchy and mobile layout. A contributes the opening explanation; C contributes a compact preview. The result is checked against the original rubric before it is accepted.

Make the comparison useful

Define the artifact and a few concrete checks. In this example: explain the product on the first screen, fit navigation at 390px, and keep the page easy to extend.
