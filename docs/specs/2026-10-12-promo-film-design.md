# Promo film: best practices from the talks, and how Harness Firmware does them

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: built at `/promo` from the audited copy deck; waiting for the owner's review. No MP4 export until the owner approves the film.

A shareable 16:9 silent film at `/promo` (`site/promo.html`, `site/promo-film.mjs`, `site/promo.css`). It opens on the owner's statement of what Harness Firmware is for, in the owner's words. Each quote beat then sets a best practice from an AI Engineer talk, in plain words with a verbatim excerpt of the speaker's quote, beside how Harness Firmware does it, with a picture of the mechanism. After the statement the open shows three problems and their answers, runs through foundations, planning, checking work, gating the pull request and improving over time, turns to three things no talk covered, shows results and closes on the human's final yes.

Every on-screen line comes word for word from the copy deck `docs/specs/2026-10-12-promo-film-copy.md`, which also cites the sources for each claim. Source for every quote and number: `docs/promo/social-proof.md`. No other quotes are used. Length: 295.0 s (4:55), the owner's choice of less text with enough time to read it.

## Rules this film follows

- Comparison, not endorsement. Each quote beat has two labelled groups side by side: "Best practice" (the practice, the quote, the speaker) and "In Harness Firmware" (tag, answer, how, why). No arrow, label or line links a speaker to Harness Firmware, and no badge claims the firmware outdoes a practice. Attribution is name and company beside the quote ("Dru Knox · Tessl"), no logos. The opening and the close carry a small line saying no speaker reviewed or endorsed Harness Firmware, and the sources list under the film says the same.
- The open starts with the owner's statement, word for word in three lines (trailing periods dropped by the display rule): "Harness Firmware aims to solve the spikiness of agent quality by steering them to work better than they normally would through reliable and repeatable methods", "Models have basically solved code. They haven't solved reliable verification", "Harness Firmware is an ongoing engineering project to attack that bottleneck". It is the owner's own voice, so it is not compressed. It reads over the dust before the machine assembles, then clears for the tagline and the three rows.
- Display copy is compressed for strangers ("caveman ultra", by the owner's choice for this film): fragments with no articles or filler, arrows for cause and sequence, only abbreviations a developer already knows (PR, CI, repo), while each line still names who does what to which file.
- Codex reviewing every pull request is stated plainly. Never claim that no pull request can merge without your approval, no skill counts, no rerun counts or other settings.
- Owner's setup items (weekly-review, disk-cleanup, token guard, Harness Console, the talk-by-talk comparison) are not shown. `/refine weekly`, which is in the template, carries the improvement beat: it opens one pull request of rule edits, and the rules change only when you merge it.
- The human step appears wherever the loop is described: you give the final yes (open, close), you correct a mistake (f3), you make each choice and pick Go (p1), you start the long job (p2), you type `/merge` (g1), you merge the rule-edit pull request (i1), you merge or type `/merge` (x1), you name the second chat and its messages never approve for you (x2).
- Display text has no closing periods and no em dashes. Quotes keep their own punctuation.
- Reading rule: each text element stays on screen until its beat ends, and the beat lasts long enough to read it and everything revealed after it at 3 words a second, plus 1 s. The one exception is the open's statement, which clears before the tagline: its three lines stay until all of it has been read at 3 words a second plus 1 s, then fade, and the tagline starts after the fade. Reveal times, the statement's clear time and beat lengths are computed from word counts, never set by hand.
- The picture is part of the claim. Whatever a readout states is true in the machine for as long as the readout shows, the machine keeps acting while the captions describe action, and nothing from the next beat appears before that beat starts.
